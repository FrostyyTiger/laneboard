### The touch user
You use the Wall with one finger, standing, no keyboard, no mouse, no hover. Judge (from the frames, the
stills and the code's input handling: read `public/js/wall/`): are targets at least ~9 mm (about 48 CSS px
at the tablet's 2x) where a finger is expected, can you reach every node or its group, does a tap give
immediate feedback, is there always an obvious way back, does the tour resume when you walk away, do
accidental touches (a palm, a sleeve) do anything harmful? If the touch explorer is not built yet, say
what the variant's layout makes easy or hard for it, concretely.
