### The operator, three metres away
You run a homelab and a handful of agent builders. The Wall hangs across the room; you glance at it
while making coffee. You do not care how it looks unless it costs you: can you tell, within three
seconds and from three metres, whether anything is wrong, what, and where? Is the worst thing the
loudest? Does the tour bring you to trouble fast, or make you wait through calm sites? Do labels at the
overview survive the distance (judge the stills at their real size: a 12-inch 3:2 tablet at 2x)?
Judge every frame of the filmstrip as a glance: what would you have known at that moment?
