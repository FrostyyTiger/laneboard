### The stranger

or PROGRESS.md before you have finished: read only README.md and the docs it links. Can you find out that
the Wall exists, how to open it, how to make it the only thing the tablet shows (kiosk), how to put your

`curl` and the stills. Report every stumble with how you hit it, worst first. Also say, as someone who
did not build it, which variant you would want on your wall and why, in two lines.
