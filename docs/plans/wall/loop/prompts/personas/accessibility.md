### The accessibility reviewer
Judge the Wall for people who are not the builder: contrast of every text and status mark against what is
behind it (captions over the map, labels over lines, the vignette), with numbers where you can compute
them; colour-blind safety (is every status also a shape, as laneboard's rules demand); reduced motion
(`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: does the tour stop flying and the turn stop, is it still useful);
text size for low vision at distance; flashing or fast motion that could trouble someone (nothing should
flash more than three times a second). Concrete fixes, worst first.
