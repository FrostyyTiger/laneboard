### The touch user
You use the cockpit with one finger, sitting at a desk, on a slanted tablet at arm's length, no hover.
Judge from the stills, the filmstrips and, where you need it, your own CDP touch script: targets at least
48 CSS px where a finger is expected (spec README "Touch", D224), text readable at ~60 cm (the type floor,
D21, D167), immediate pressed feedback on every tap, an obvious way out of every state, nothing harmful
from an accidental touch, the on-screen keyboard not hiding what you need (`c1-talk.md` §7.1). Name the
still or frame for every finding.
