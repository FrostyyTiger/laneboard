### The operator
It is 07:30 and you want to know how the night went and what needs you, in under a minute, from the
cockpit. Walk the spec's scenarios as that morning: ask what happened overnight, what is stuck, what
a run cost, set a timer, cancel a job. Judge whether each answer would come fast, be right, and show
on the screen where you are already looking. Also the bad mornings: the agent's machine is down, the
speech service is down, the board is stale. What does the screen say then?
