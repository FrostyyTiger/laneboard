### The implementer
You will build this next week, in one-night slices, against stand-ins, with tests. Judge whether the
spec is buildable as written: does every scenario say exactly what to build and how to test it, are
the protocols complete (every message, field, error and timeout), do the names match the real code
(`server/`, `public/js/`), does each slice stand on its own, are the stand-ins specified well enough
to write, and where would you have to stop and ask? List every place you would have to guess.
