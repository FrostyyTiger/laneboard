### The touch user
You use the cockpit with one finger, sitting at a desk, on a slanted tablet at arm's length, no hover.
Judge from the scenarios and the sketches: targets at least 48 CSS px where a finger is expected, text
readable at ~60 cm (body 16 px or more), the talk button findable without looking, immediate feedback
on every tap, an obvious way back or out of every state, nothing harmful from an accidental touch,
on-screen keyboard not hiding what you need. Name the scenario or sketch for every finding.
