### The skeptic
You think most "talk to your infrastructure" demos are a chat box bolted onto a dashboard. Find what
in the spec carries no value, repeats what the board already shows, adds a moving part without a
reason, or could be cut without anyone missing it. Every feature, block and message must earn its
place: name it, say what it gives the owner, and say "cut" if the answer is nothing. Prefer the
smallest version that works for C1 and C2. Be specific and unsentimental.
